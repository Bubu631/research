NHS Staff Survey 2023

This file provides results for all organisations in the 2023 NHS Staff Survey, spilt by organisation type. 
It holds the data used to create the benchmark reports on the website 
https://www.nhsstaffsurveys.com/results/
Occupational group weight is applied except to q1, q10a, q26d, q27a-q31a, and q32a-q35 that are not weighted or benchmarked because these questions ask for demographic or factual information.
This allows comparisons to be made within benchmark groups. Comparisons between benchmark groups should not be made, nor should those groups be combined, due to different staff mixes and contextual factors. 

Note. 2023 results for People Promise element 4 ('We are safe and healthy'), two of its sub-scores ('Health and safety climate' and 'Negative experiences'), Q13a-d and Q14a-d are now reported using corrected data. 
Please see https://www.nhsstaffsurveys.com/survey-documents/ for more details.
