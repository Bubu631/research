## Novelty and evidence audit

For Paper A, the defensible contribution is a diagnostic specialization: exact edited/untouched gradient geometry, target mismatch under localization, and reproducible measurements with explicit failure cases. Importance weighting, probability-reference-sample identification, and integrand-dependent variance are prior work. The recent span-feedback and sparse-feedback preprints are real, but neither demonstrates that this manuscript solves operational complaint identification. A nonlinear preference loss must not be claimed covered without a separate decomposition.

For Paper B, Learn then Test and Seldonian work already contain the selection/testing separation; model interpolation and Pareto merging also predate this study. Provably Safe Model Updates already treats no-op anchors, safe parameter regions, and finite-sample uncertainty. The current manuscript correctly frames its contribution as a paired incumbent-relative release protocol and controlled study, not a new general concentration theorem or universally better merging operator. A comparison against other methods has to use their actual candidates and the same independent certification data; a literature paragraph is not an empirical baseline.

No nonexistent cited paper was found. The material corrections concern publication status, author order, and bibliographic precision, together with removal of overbroad novelty claims. Direct HTTP requests to several publisher sites returned 403; accessible publisher-indexed records, author copies, and arXiv metadata were used instead. No access control was bypassed. Publication status for the two recent Paper A preprints remains unasserted beyond arXiv. The audit does not establish exhaustive coverage of all related work or independent human approval of the manuscripts.
